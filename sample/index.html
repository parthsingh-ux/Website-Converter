Common questions

What Kamloops-area buyers ask before they hire us

Can one contractor handle our full scope?

Yes. We take a commercial project from cleared ground through excavation, utility servicing, grading, demolition and finished site, plus aggregate from our own pits, so you're not coordinating separate crews for each stage.

Do you own your equipment or subcontract?

We run our own fleet of more than 50 pieces of earth-moving equipment, including McCloskey C2 cone crushers and S130 2-deck screeners. The machines on your site are ours, not rented in for the season.

Are your operators certified and safety trained?

Our operators are experienced and safety certified, running heavy iron on live sites where mistakes are costly. That certification sits behind more than 56 years of Hall family ground work in the Kamloops area.

Do you work with commercial clients?

Most of our work is commercial — builders, developers and site managers across the Kamloops area. We also handle residential lots and basements, run by the same operators who take on our largest commercial jobs.

Which areas do you serve?

Kamloops and the surrounding area. Half a century working ground in one market means we've dug, graded and demolished in conditions like yours before, so the work gets done right the first time.
